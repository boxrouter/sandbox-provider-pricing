UsageRates: micro-units (1e-6) of the org's currency per unit-hour.
Fields: vcpu_hour_micro, ram_gib_hour_micro, disk_gib_hour_micro (integers).
MachineUsageReport: machine_id, status, resources, seconds, vcpu_hours,
ram_gib_hours, disk_gib_hours, cost_micro, complete, period, currency, rates.
Currency is lower-case ISO 4217: "eur" or "usd".
UsagePeriod has start and end (exclusive), in 15-minute buckets.

Verbatim cost_micro documentation:
Estimated cost at current rates, micro-units of the currency
(``cost_micro / 1e6`` is the amount). Billing activity is what was charged.

Verbatim complete documentation:
All of this machine's usage in the window has been metered, so the
figures won't grow. Metering lags by up to ~25 minutes: a machine read
right after ``delete()`` is not complete yet, and one that still exists
never is in a window that runs to now. Store a cost once this is True.

Limits: synthetic fixtures test record interpretation only. They do not prove
billing reconciliation, retention or finality on a live account.
