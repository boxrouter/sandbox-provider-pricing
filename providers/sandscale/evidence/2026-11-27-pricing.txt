On paid plans you choose what happens when your included hours run out: turn usage beyond them on at $0.05 per awake vCPU-hour, add an hour pack of 500 awake vCPU-hours for $10, or keep it off and new boxes wait for next month.
One vCPU running for one hour while the box is awake. A box that is paused, or idle between tool calls, does not count. A 2 vCPU agent box open for 10 hours and awake 10% of the time uses 2 awake vCPU-hours.
Every vCPU comes with 4 GiB of memory, included in the price. Memory above that counts as extra vCPU-hours: 4 GiB-hours of extra memory is 1 vCPU-hour.
Burst comes from the same hours and counts only the CPU actually used above the base: a minute at 8 vCPU uses 8 vCPU-minutes.
Pro for one developer $20/mo billed annually or $25/mo billed monthly
Team for teams $40/member/mo billed annually, or $48 monthly
A monthly or yearly subscription in US dollars, by card through Stripe, with invoices and tax. There are no prepaid credits and no pay as you go.
On Free, new boxes wait until next month.
One allowance covers disks (stopped workspaces included), snapshots, templates and objects: Free 5 GB, Pro 50 GB, Team 100 GB per member, pooled. Above that it is $0.15 per GB-month. The first 10 GB of snapshots and templates is free.
